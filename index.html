About Us

FTC Team 17113

We are Hunga Munga 17113, an FTC Robotics team from Westwood High School in Austin, Texas. Our 15 members are passionate about engineering, designing, and innovating.

Our mission is to build a strong engineering community and inspire the next generation of creators, thinkers, and innovators.
